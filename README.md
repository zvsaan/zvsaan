## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🌾 Staff Monitoring & Agritech Mapping System

Platform for monitoring field staff activities, mapping farmer land using GeoPolygon, managing monthly targets, demographic data, and supporting offline mode on Android applications.

**Tech Stack:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Prisma ORM` `PostgreSQL` `Flutter`

---

### 💡 SIGAP — Public Street Lighting Management System

Geographic information system for managing thousands of public street-lighting assets, electrical panels, public complaints, asset conditions, and WhatsApp notification integration.

**Tech Stack:** `Next.js` `TypeScript` `Prisma ORM` `PostgreSQL` `Leaflet` `PostGIS`

---

### ⚙️ QiuParts — Heavy Equipment Spare-Part ERP

Enterprise system for managing domestic and international spare-part purchasing, purchase orders, vendors, inventory, landed cost, sales, receivables, and accounting processes.

**Tech Stack:** `Laravel` `PHP` `PostgreSQL` `Bootstrap`

---

### 📦 ERP Inventory & Payroll System

Integrated enterprise application for managing inventory, purchasing, transactions, employee attendance, payroll calculations, reporting, and role-based access control.

**Tech Stack:** `Laravel` `PostgreSQL` `REST API`

---

### 🏠 Interactive 3D Room Customizer

Web-based visualization application that allows users to interactively customize room layouts, materials, colors, and three-dimensional objects.

**Tech Stack:** `Laravel` `Three.js` `WebGL` `JavaScript`
